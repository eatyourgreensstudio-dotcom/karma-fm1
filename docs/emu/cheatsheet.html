KARMA cheat sheet

Where everything lives on KARMA, the free firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, and how to reach it. Tap a button to open its pages (tap again or turn SELECT for the next one); hold a button for what it does with the keys and knobs.

Tracks 2 and 3

ELEMENTS (track 2)

  • MODEL: BOUNC (bouncing hits), PICK (plectrum), BOW (bowed, holds while you hold), BLOW (breath, a reed pipe above ⅔), OMIN (the hidden Ominous Voice: RATIO FM FDBK DTUNE BRIT SHAPE SPACE; one note).
  • BOW: the BOW knob is pressure, TONE the bow noise. Play harder for a rougher bow that speaks sooner.

PLAITS (track 3)

  • MODEL (24): FOLD, SWARM, WAVE, HARM, CHORD, VA, VCF, PHDIS, FM, 6OP A/B/C (DX banks: HARM picks the patch), GRAIN, TERRN, STRMC, CHIP, STRNG, MODAL, NOISE, SPECH, PARTC, KICK, SNARE, HIHAT.
  • Knobs HARM, TIMB, MRPH (as on the module), ENV, SUB. CHORD: the chord name shows on the knob.
  • Big models (6OP, STRMC, STRNG, SPECH, PARTC) play one note at a time; the small ones up to four.

Track 1 plays ACID or JUNO, track 2 ELEMENTS, track 3 PLAITS, the drums HEX kits: PRESETS shows each track's own sounds.

  • Turning GEN ON writes a new part at once.
  • A key pressed on the GEN page (FM-1 keys or a MIDI keyboard) turns GEN on for that track and sets the KEY to that note: every GEN track follows.

GEN roles each track plays its part of the band

TRACK 1 · BASSOne acid rhythm repeated every bar, the chord's root at each chord change, accents and slides.
TRACK 2 · LINELong chord notes, a new one each bar, moving smoothly. A short struck ELEMENTS sound is re-struck on the beats.
TRACK 3 · LEADA one-bar motif developed over the loop, chord tones on the beats, ending on the home note.

The drum voices come from the open-source LXR-02 firmware by Erica Synths (GPL-3.0), ported to KARMA: its drum, snare, cymbal and hi-hat voices and its short click transients. CLAP is the snare voice with repeats.

The built-in kits never change: your first edit copies the kit into MY HEX 1–4 and switches to it. Saved on the FM-1 by itself.

The FM-1 is a class-compliant USB audio interface and a USB MIDI device: no drivers.

MIDI keyboard e.g. Akai MPK mini

  • Keys on channel 1 play the selected track (GLO › MIDI 2 › IN = CH 1). Channels 2, 3 still play tracks 2, 3; channel 10 (the pads) the drums.
  • On the GEN page a key turns GEN on and sets the KEY; on the HARMONY page it sets the KEY only.
  • Knobs K1–K8 are the FM-1's eight rotaries (GLO › MIDI 3 › KNOBS = PANEL): K1 PRESETS (the sounds), K2 SELECT (pages), K3 ALGORITHM (tracks), K4 MASTER, K5–K8 KNOB 1–4 (whatever the screen shows). KNOBS = AUTO / 1-8 / 70-77: the EDIT knobs instead; GM: the old map.
  • Any other CC: MIDI LEARN (hold GLO + F#3, touch a knob, move the controller).

What is saved

  • Project (hold SAVE, or SAVE → PROJECT): patterns, sounds, the KEY and MODE, CLOUD settings and sends, each drum sound's reverb and delay.
  • MY HEX 1–4 drum kits: saved on the FM-1 by themselves, for every project.
  • Not saved: GEN on / off, HARMONY's PROG and BARS, the ACID GEN knobs. What they wrote is your pattern and is saved.
