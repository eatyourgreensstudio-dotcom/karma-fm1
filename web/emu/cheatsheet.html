Track 2 and 3 engines

ELEMENTS (track 2)

  • MODEL: BOUNC (bouncing hits), PICK (plectrum), BOW (bowed, holds while you hold), BLOW (breath, a reed pipe above ⅔).
  • BOW: the BOW knob is pressure, TONE the bow noise. Play harder for a rougher bow that speaks sooner.

PLAITS (track 3)

  • MODEL: FOLD, SWARM, WAVE, HARM, CHORD. Knobs HARM, TIMB, MRPH, ENV, SUB.
  • CHORD: the chord name shows on the knob.

Any track can play any engine: browse with PRESETS.

Effects tap FX

  • Pages: FX (DST CHO DLY REV) · FILTER · SLICER · INSERT · INSERT 2 · DLY · REV/CHO · CLOUD · CLOUD 2.
  • Hold FX + a key: punch-in effects on the whole mix while the key is held.

What is saved

  • Project (SAVE → PROJECT): patterns, sounds, CLOUD settings and sends, each drum sound's reverb and delay.
  • MY HEX 1–4 drum kits: saved on the FM-1 by themselves, for every project.
  • Not saved: ACID GEN knob settings (the line they wrote is).
