Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

Free, open-source firmware for the FM-1: four tracks you play live. An acid bass or a Juno-style polysynth, physical modelling, two dozen synthesis models and a drum synthesizer, a 64-step sequencer with a song mode, generators that keep writing parts on one chord plan while you play, and a cast of pixel characters that react to every note.

4tracks: ACID or JUNO, ELEMENTS, PLAITS, HEX drums
64steps a track, chords, song mode
GENwrites parts on one chord plan
USBaudio, six stems, MIDI, DAW sync
